COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Are these PDF tools free to use?

Yes. The tools included in this starter website are available without an account. Your hosting and internet provider may have their own charges.

Are my documents uploaded?

The included tools process selected files locally in your browser. The site code does not upload those files to a server. Third-party hosting, browser extensions, or changes you make to the code may affect privacy, so review your deployed setup.

Can every PDF be processed?

Not always. Password-protected, damaged, unusually large, or complex PDFs may fail. PDF-to-image rendering also depends on your device memory and browser support.

Will this website be approved for AdSense?

No website can guarantee approval. Add original helpful content, publish accurate policy pages, test all tools, provide clear navigation, and review the current Google AdSense requirements before applying.
